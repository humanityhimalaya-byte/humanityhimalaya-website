Get Involved

Change begins when people come together. Whether you volunteer, donate, fundraise, partner with us, or simply spread the word, your support can help communities recover, rebuild, and move forward.

There Are Many Ways to Help

You do not have to do everything. Choose the way that works best for you and become part of a community committed to helping people in need.

🤝

Volunteer

Give your time, skills, and energy to support Humanity Himalaya and the communities we serve.

  • Help with community activities
  • Support fundraising events
  • Assist with outreach and awareness
  • Share your professional skills
❤️

Donate

Your contribution can help provide essential support to people and communities affected by hardship and disasters.

  • Every contribution matters
  • Support relief efforts
  • Help communities rebuild
  • Make a direct difference
📣

Fundraise

Turn your network, event, or personal passion into an opportunity to help others.

  • Organize a fundraising event
  • Create a community campaign
  • Raise funds with friends and family
  • Support a specific relief effort
🏢

Sponsor

Businesses, banks, organizations, and community groups can help create a larger and lasting impact.

  • Sponsor a relief initiative
  • Support community events
  • Provide financial or in-kind support
  • Build a long-term partnership
🌍

Partner With Us

We welcome organizations and community groups that share our commitment to humanitarian support and community resilience.

  • Community organizations
  • Nonprofit organizations
  • Businesses and institutions
  • Local and international partners
📱

Spread the Word

Awareness is powerful. Help more people discover our mission and learn how they can make a difference.

  • Share our website
  • Share our campaigns
  • Tell your community about us
  • Follow and share our updates
